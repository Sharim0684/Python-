Data Deletion Policy

We respect your privacy and provide you with control over your data. If you wish to delete your data from our platform, please follow the instructions below.

How to Request Data Deletion

If you would like to delete your data, please send an email to info@innovatorstech.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and include your account details.

Automated Deletion

If our platform supports automated deletion, you can visit your account settings and select the "Delete My Account" option.

Facebook Data Deletion

If you have logged in using Facebook and wish to delete your data associated with our platform, please follow these steps:

  1. Go to Facebook Settings.
  2. Click on "Apps and Websites" and find our app.
  3. Select "Remove" and confirm deletion of any data associated with our platform.

For any concerns or queries, feel free to contact us.
